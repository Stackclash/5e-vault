# Death God's Touch
*7th-level, Necromancy*  

- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

This spell allows you to shred the life force of a creature you touch. You become [[/5. Mechanics/Rules/Conditions.md#Invisible\|invisible]] and make a melee spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes `dice:10d10|noform|noparens|avg` (`10d10`) necrotic damage. If this damage reduces the target to 0 hit points, the target dies. Whether the attack hits or misses, you remain [[/5. Mechanics/Rules/Conditions.md#Invisible\|invisible]] until the start of your next turn.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 8th level or higher, the damage increases by `dice:2d10|noform|noparens|avg` (`2d10`) for each slot level above 7th.
