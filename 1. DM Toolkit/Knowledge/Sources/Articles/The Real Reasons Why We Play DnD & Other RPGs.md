# Document Note

Background: I am a dungeon master seeking practical ways to improve my GMing skills and run more engaging, consistent, and satisfying tabletop RPG sessions.

🎭  Consistency in world rules — Ensure mechanics, magic, and NPC reactions behave predictably. Establish and apply consistent rules and consequences so players can suspend disbelief and engage in the story.

🧭  Use clear narrative structure — Run sessions with a beginning, middle, and end (or scene beats) so pacing and player expectations feel satisfying and familiar, borrowing structure from other forms of entertainment.

🤝  Focus on player motivations — Build play around the four core drives: entertainment, escapism, success, and socialization. Tailor challenges, rewards, and table dynamics to satisfy those motivations for your group.
