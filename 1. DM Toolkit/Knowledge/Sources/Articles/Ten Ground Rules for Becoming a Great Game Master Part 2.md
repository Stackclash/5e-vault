# Document Note

Background: I am a dungeon master actively trying to improve my skills and techniques to run better, more engaging tabletop RPG sessions.

🔁

🎯 Embrace learning from other RPG systems — study their core mechanics and solutions (e.g., aerial/combat, hacking, starship rules) and selectively adapt those mechanics to enrich your fantasy games.

🔁

🛠️ Accept mistakes and iterate — expect to make errors, treat them as fixable, move on quickly, and use play experience to refine your approach rather than chasing perfection.

🔁

🤝 Set and communicate play expectations — clarify what you need to have fun (backstory, contributions from players, narrative rewards) and hold players accountable so the table collaborates toward a shared fun experience.
