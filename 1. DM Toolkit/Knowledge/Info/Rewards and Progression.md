# Hope-Giving Rewards
After a major setback, the reward phase should give the party both hope and practical help for what comes next.[^1][^2] That reward can take the form of knowledge, an artifact, an ally, or another boon that helps them regroup, rethink their situation, and begin forming a plan for the final confrontation.[^1][^3] In this way, the reward is not just a prize for surviving Act Two, but part of how the campaign restores momentum and makes the last stretch feel possible.[^2][^3]
