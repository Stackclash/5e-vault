# Inciting Incident
Define why the campaign begins at this specific moment. Identify the inciting incident that makes this point in the world matter, and make sure the players have a clear reason to care about it. The opening should answer why this is the moment the story truly begins.[^1]

# Session One Ending
Consider holding the inciting incident until the end of session one so the first session ends on a strong turning point that creates momentum for the rest of the campaign.[^2]
