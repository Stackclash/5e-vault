# Revealing Setting Information
Introduce the world with only enough detail to establish tone and spark curiosity, leaving much of it to be uncovered gradually through play rather than explained all at once.[^1] Early setting information should give players a feel for the world and the story’s vibe without relying on exposition dumps unless they are truly necessary.[^1]
