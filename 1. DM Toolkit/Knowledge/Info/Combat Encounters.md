# Designing the Final Battle
The final battle should feel epic in scale and pay off the campaign by incorporating allied support, the party’s chosen strategies, and the personal growth of the characters.[^1] It should also include moments that highlight each character’s individual strengths or teamwork so the climax feels earned and centered on this party.[^2]
