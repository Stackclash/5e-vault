# Document Note

Background: I am a dungeon master who wants to become a better, more engaging, and less predictable DM.

🧭  Know the prior patterns and tropes players expect — study past modules and common D&D clichés so you can deliberately subvert them or offer fresh alternatives.

🎯  Design encounters and story beats with variety and purpose — avoid repeating the same types of scenes; replace overused setups with novel challenges that still serve the campaign’s goals.

🔁  Actively track what your table has seen and done — use that knowledge to tailor surprises, escalate stakes appropriately, and keep each session feeling new rather than recycled.
