# Document Note

Background: I am a Dungeon Master seeking practical advice to improve my GMing skills and run better, more fun tabletop RPG sessions.

🎯  
Everybody (including NPCs/monsters) should have clear goals — ensure player characters and adversaries have motives so scenes feel purposeful and players can make meaningful decisions.

🏃‍♂️  
Don’t be afraid to retreat or surrender — adjust encounters and outcomes (including running away or ending fights early) to preserve fun and story momentum rather than forcing lethal, drawn-out combat.

⚖️  
Use rules as context, not scripture — understand core mechanics and their intent, then tweak numbers or house rules to fit your table’s mix and keep play enjoyable; the GM is a player among players, and the story is a collective responsibility.
