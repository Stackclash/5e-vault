  Professor Gousse gathered the Umbral Athenaeum and the party worked through them one at a time in a side room — cast something, answer a few questions, next. A nervous dwarf broke under the pressure and confessed to running the Silent Ascendency's cell in Bruokdon. Before that could go anywhere, a guard pushed in with word for Lord Velmont: Corwin Vantor had been found dead.

  Roark heard it and stormed out to find Lyssandra, with Faerah after him. Velmont left to see the scene for himself, and Wisp followed him unseen, sending a spider familiar ahead — Corwin's body, and the real detection device crushed beneath it. In the street, guards stopped Roark and Faerah and demanded they come along. When they refused, steel came out — and then Roark turned on Faerah. By the end of it the guards were dead, and so was Roark, cut down by Faerah in single combat. The corpse at her feet wasn't Roark: it was the doppelganger that had worn Lord Velmont's face and then Gousse's, with an amber gem lodged at the base of its neck. The party left the body where it fell to come back to.

  Wisp searched the dwarf's room and turned up papers on a shipment leaving Bruokdon that same day. Behind an elaborate screen of Wisp's illusions, the party turned the wagon off its route and broke it open — a load of smuggled magic items, and, alive, Mistress Lyssandra Veil and the real Roark Battleborn. A cultist taken at the wagon gave them a name for the thing Faerah had killed: Marrow.

## Key Learnings
- The device "Roark" brought back from the [[Corwin Vantor]] handoff was a fake — Corwin's real prototype was destroyed at the ambush site #remember
- [[Corwin Vantor]] is dead, killed defending the device #remember
- The [[Doppelganger]] that impersonated [[Lord Caldris Velmont]] and then [[Professor Gousse Windcaller]] in S43 had been wearing [[3. The Party/Players/Roark Battleborn|Roark Battleborn]] since the ambush, and is now dead #remember
- The doppelganger's name was [[Marrow]] — given up by a cultist taken at the wagon #remember
- [[Marrow]] carried an amber gem lodged at the base of its neck. The party has not investigated it, or recovered the body #remember
- The Silent Ascendency was still running shipments out of [[Bruokdon]] along [[Lord Caldris Velmont|Velmont's]] old smuggling routes after his exposure — captives moved alongside smuggled magic items #remember
- The nameless dwarf's confession to leading the Bruokdon cell was a lie and a frame; [[Marrow]] was the cell leader #remember #secret

## Items Of Importance
- An amber gem lodged at the base of [[Marrow|Marrow's]] neck — left with the body, uninvestigated
- The fake heartbeat-detection device "Roark" returned with — inert, never functional
- Documents from the dwarf's room detailing a shipment leaving [[Bruokdon]] that day
- A wagonload of smuggled magic items recovered with the captives (no loot distributed)

## What Happened
- [[Professor Gousse Windcaller]] rounded up [[The Umbral Athenaeum]], and the party took members one at a time into a side room to test and question them.
- Standing from days earlier: [[3. The Party/Players/Roark Battleborn|Roark Battleborn]] and [[Mistress Lyssandra Veil]] had left to collect the heartbeat-detection device from [[Corwin Vantor]]. "Roark" returned with the device and without her.
- Partway through the testing, a nervous dwarf cracked under pressure and confessed to being the ringleader of the Silent Ascendency's cell in [[Bruokdon]]. (Untrue — a setup.)
- A guard ran in with word for [[Lord Caldris Velmont]] that [[Corwin Vantor|Corwin Vantor's]] body had been found.
- "Roark" overheard, stormed out to look for [[Mistress Lyssandra Veil|Lyssandra]], and [[3. The Party/Players/Faerah Duskrane|Faerah Duskrane]] followed him.
- [[Lord Caldris Velmont]] left immediately to investigate the scene himself. [[3. The Party/Players/Wisp|Wisp]] followed him unseen and used a spider familiar to get eyes on [[Corwin Vantor|Corwin's]] body — the real device broken underneath it.
- Guards stopped "Roark" and [[3. The Party/Players/Faerah Duskrane|Faerah]] in the street and insisted they come along. When they refused, the guards drew their swords — and "Roark" turned on [[3. The Party/Players/Faerah Duskrane|Faerah]].
- The two fought. [[3. The Party/Players/Faerah Duskrane|Faerah]] killed him, and the body reverted to its true [[Doppelganger]] form. The guards were killed in the fighting.
- An amber gem was visible lodged at the base of the doppelganger's neck. The party left the body in place to investigate later.
- [[3. The Party/Players/Wisp|Wisp]] searched the dwarf's room and found documents referencing a shipment leaving [[Bruokdon]] that day. The dwarf was taken into custody.
- Using an elaborate set of illusions from [[3. The Party/Players/Wisp|Wisp]], the party redirected the wagon carrying the shipment and freed [[Mistress Lyssandra Veil]] and the real [[3. The Party/Players/Roark Battleborn|Roark Battleborn]], along with a load of smuggled magic items.
- A cultist taken at the wagon named the doppelganger: [[Marrow]], the Bruokdon cell's leader.
