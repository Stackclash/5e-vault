summary: |-
  The party pushed downriver from Bruokdon to Duskferry, carrying Hesper Crane's ledger as their cover story. Getting into town wasn't as quiet as planned — a checkpoint flagged their manifest, and Wisp's illusion made it look like it had blown away in the wind, buying the dock workers time to draw up a fresh one instead. With the Guildhall Roost already closed for the night, getting it notarized had to wait until morning, so the party settled in at The Dapper Helm to wait out the fog.

  The layover became its own kind of night. Faerah Duskrane crossed paths with Nimrie, a drow woman, who brought her to Naerissa, an elder of a small dusk elf community tending a weathered stone shrine to Eilistraee at the edge of town. Faerah and Naerissa prayed together before the shrine, and the goddess herself answered — a message meant only for Faerah, tied to the pendant she's worn since Barovia.

  By morning the fog had thinned enough to travel, and the party took to the water again, poling downriver through marsh and dead trees toward Greyfen Hollow. A second night passed on the boat before the trees finally closed in — grey trunks rising out of black water, and no sound of anything living. The session ends with the party on the threshold of Greyfen Hollow itself.
