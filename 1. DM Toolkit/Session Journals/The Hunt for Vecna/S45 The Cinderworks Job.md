summary: |-
  Two paths opened at once — Gousse's planar route to the Great Library, and Velmont's window to walk back into his own smuggling network before anyone noticed his face hadn't changed. The party chose Velmont's play: find out where the rest of the smuggled shipment was actually going.

  Before leaving Bruokdon, the party stopped by [[Professor Alindra Quicksilver]] at the Umbral Athenaeum to stock up for the job — potions of healing, Salamander Fire, and a dose of Dust of Disappearance.

  Wisp worked through the papers recovered from the Bruokdon cell and traced the trail to The Cinderworks, a disused foundry outside the city — the first waypoint for the Ascendency's magic items leaving Bruokdon. The party built a plan around Velmont resuming the operation Marrow had been running in his name: Velmont went in with Roark Battleborn and Yevelda Ovak posing as his hired muscle, Faerah Duskrane and Wisp took the roof to ambush from above, and Redgrave hid in the wagon meant to carry the "recovered" shipment.

  Inside, Velmont's quartermaster contact Hesper Crane met them on the foundry floor, ledger in hand, already pressing about the missing shipment. A distraction from Velmont opened the fight, and the party overwhelmed and captured the workers before anyone could raise an alarm. Crane herself was taken alive and interrogated — she gave up the name of the Ferrymen's Guild contact waiting upriver at Duskferry, known only as "the Tallyman," along with the code phrase needed to approach him: "The day has been long, but the evening even longer."

  With the foundry secured, the party searched the loft office and turned up a small stockpile: a cloak of the manta ray, a pair of sending stones, a ring of protection, a second dose of dust of disappearance, and a locked crate holding a strange amber-cored device nobody could immediately identify.
