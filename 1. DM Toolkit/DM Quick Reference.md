## Improvisation Toolkit

### Quick NPC Generator
When you need an NPC on the fly, roll or choose:

| d6 | Demeanor | Quirk | Want |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Friendly | Speaks in whispers | Coin or payment |
| 2 | Suspicious | Constantly fidgeting | Information |
| 3 | Nervous | Avoids eye contact | Protection |
| 4 | Boisterous | Laughs at everything | Recognition |
| 5 | Stoic | Cracks knuckles | Revenge |
| 6 | Sly | Overly formal speech | An escape |

### Random Encounter Complications
Add these to any encounter to make it memorable:

| d8 | Complication |
|---|---|
| 1 | A third party arrives with their own agenda |
| 2 | The environment shifts (weather, collapse, fire) |
| 3 | An ally is secretly working against the party |
| 4 | A timer: something bad happens in d4 rounds |
| 5 | The enemy has a hostage or leverage |
| 6 | The battlefield has a dangerous hazard |
| 7 | Reinforcements are on the way for both sides |
| 8 | A moral dilemma: the enemy has a sympathetic motive |

### Scene Pacing Guide
- **If players seem lost:** Reveal a secret or clue for free
- **If combat drags:** Add a complication or environmental change
- **If players are cautious:** Have the enemy act first
- **If a session is winding down:** End on a cliffhanger or revelation
- **If roleplay stalls:** Have an NPC ask a direct question or make a request

## Narrative Tools

### The Three Clue Rule
For any critical plot point, prepare at least **three** clues that lead to the same conclusion. Players will miss the first, misinterpret the second, and find the third.

### Fronts (Stolen from Dungeon World)
For each active threat or faction, define:
- **Danger:** What is this threat?
- **Impulse:** What does it want to do?
- **Grim Portent 1-3:** What steps does it take if unchecked?
- **Impending Doom:** What happens if the party doesn't intervene?

### Yes, And / Yes, But
- **"Yes, and..."** — The action succeeds and something extra happens
- **"Yes, but..."** — The action succeeds with a complication
- **"No, but..."** — The action fails but something positive comes from it
- **"No, and..."** — The action fails and something else goes wrong

## Combat Reference

### Encounter Difficulty (Quick Math)
- **Easy:** Total monster CR ≤ Party level total ÷ 4
- **Medium:** Total monster CR ≈ Party level total ÷ 2
- **Hard:** Total monster CR ≈ Party level total × 0.75
- **Deadly:** Total monster CR ≥ Party level total

### Minion Rules (Optional)
For cinematic combat with many weak enemies:
- **Minions have 1 HP** — any hit kills them
- **Minions deal static damage** (no rolling)
- **Minions never require saving throws** — they auto-fail
- **Group minions:** 5 minions act as one, dealing 5× damage on a hit

### Morale Checks (Optional)
When enemies face setbacks, consider having them flee:
- Leader killed → DC 10 WIS save or flee
- Half the group killed → DC 12 WIS save or flee
- Clearly outmatched → DC 15 WIS save or flee

## World Building Quick Tools

### Settlement Features
When players visit an unplanned town, decide:

| Aspect | d6 Options |
|---|---|
| **Notable Feature** | 1: Grand temple 2: Market square 3: Ruined tower 4: Fighting pit 5: Library 6: Haunted building |
| **Current Problem** | 1: Bandits nearby 2: Plague/sickness 3: Missing people 4: Political tension 5: Monster sightings 6: Resource shortage |
| **Local Authority** | 1: Elected mayor 2: Noble family 3: Religious leader 4: Military commander 5: Council of elders 6: Crime boss |

### Treasure Beyond Gold
Make rewards more interesting:
- **Information:** Maps, letters, coded journals, prophecies
- **Favors:** A noble owes you, a guild offers services, a spirit grants a boon
- **Property:** Deed to a building, a ship, a business
- **Titles:** Knighthood, guild membership, noble title
- **Story Hooks:** Cursed items, mysterious keys, incomplete ritual components

## Useful Frontmatter Fields for Note Types

### NPC Notes — Consider Adding
- `motivation:` — What drives this NPC right now?
- `secret:` — What are they hiding?
- `voiceNotes:` — Accent, cadence, catchphrases
- `lastInteraction:` — When did the party last meet them?

### Location Notes — Consider Adding
- `mood:` — The overall atmosphere (eerie, bustling, sacred)
- `sounds:` — Ambient audio description
- `hazards:` — Environmental dangers
- `hiddenFeatures:` — Secrets the players haven't discovered

### Session Journal — Consider Adding
- `xpAwarded:` — Track experience per session
- `goldSpent:` — Economic tracking
- `decisionsToTrack:` — Key player choices with future consequences
- `openThreads:` — Unresolved plot hooks from this session
