## Strong Start: "Leave."

> [!quote] What They See
> The pool lights him from below. Green, crackling, never quite still: thin threads of discharge snap across the surface and die, and every time they do, the shadows on the dome jump. He's bigger than you remember. The left arm is the same red, barbed thing you knew in Vallaki, but it doesn't stop at the shoulder anymore. It has grown up his neck and down his ribs in plates that don't sit flush. At the end of it, where the claws should be, is a hand that doesn't match the rest of him at all: black, shriveled, mummified, like something taken out of a tomb. Under his skin, thin as thread, the same amber light runs that you've been watching crawl through the pipes all day.
>
> He doesn't reach for the axe.

> [!quote] Izek Strazni
> "Leave."
>
> *(a long pause)*
>
> "Now."

He doesn't explain or threaten, and he won't say it a third time. If they talk, he gives them almost nothing. See the Important NPCs section.

### The other voice

When it's clear they aren't leaving, the green veining in the arm **flares**, and Izek tilts his head toward the black hand like a man listening to someone at his shoulder. The party hears **only his side** of the conversation. Leave a pause after each line for the voice nobody else can hear.

> [!quote] Izek, to no one
> "They're nobody."
>
> "…They were kind to me."
>
> "I know what I owe."
>
> *(longer)* "Not her."
>
> *(he closes his eyes)* "…Yes."

Then he looks at [[Yevelda Ovak]], a beat too long, the way the earlier notes have him do.

> [!quote] Izek Strazni
> "I'm sorry."

Roll initiative.

**What the players can catch, no roll needed:** the hand is shriveled and black and doesn't match the arm; the veins flare whenever he pauses; he's answering someone. **DC 15 Arcana or History** (or [[Wisp]]'s player asking the right question): stories of the Whispered One say all that's left of his body is **one hand and one eye**, and that they're still out there in the world. Let them say "the Hand of Vecna" out loud before you confirm it. A player already guessed it.

## The Fight: The Reservoir

> [!info] Lazy DM benchmark
> 5 PCs × level 10 = 50 summed levels. **Potentially deadly above summed CR 25.**
> - **Phase 1: Izek alone + the pool.** CR **17**. Under the line on paper. In practice it's harder: he's a legendary solo, the pool hits every round, and the party is running on fumes. This should be the hardest fight of the campaign so far.
> - **The recall round:** Izek only uses Unwritten Step, so the overlap with the Wardens arriving doesn't stack.
> - **Phase 2: the Wardens + the pool.** [[Amber Warden]] 10 + [[Unfinished Amber Warden]] 5 = **15**.
> - **Dial up** (they rested, or Phase 1 went easily): a second Unfinished Warden brings it to **20**.
> - **Dial down** (two or more PCs down at the trigger): the Unfinished Warden stays jammed in its cradle. That leaves **10**.

### Terrain
- **The Unmaking Pool**, center of the dome. Wading costs extra movement. Entering it or starting a turn in it deals **22 (4d10) force**, plus a **DC 16 Con** save or a random magic item goes dark until dawn.
- **Surges:** roll a d4 on initiative 20 each round, or Izek picks one with *Stir the Pool*:
  1. Arc-lash: 3d10 force, DC 16 Dex, up to three creatures within 10 ft. of the edge.
  2. Undertow: DC 16 Str or pulled 10 ft. toward the pool.
  3. Hunger: concentration saves at disadvantage within 30 ft. until the next initiative 20. **This applies to Izek too.**
  4. Faces Below: DC 16 Str or restrained, for anyone already in the pool.
- **Six glyph-stones** in a ring around the basin give **half cover** (AC 17, 30 HP each). Breaking three starts the **ten-round collapse** from [[Greyfen Hollow]]. If that happens mid-fight, the fight becomes a race.
- **The casks** on pallets along the far wall give three-quarters cover. If a cask takes 15+ damage in one hit, it bursts into a **Raw Magic Vent**.
- **Izek's Watch (D2)** is a ledge above the basin. Izek can reach it with *Unwritten Step*; the party has to climb (DC 15 Athletics).

### Who's at risk (the Whispered Truth uses each target's *lowest* save)
| PC | Lowest save | vs DC 18 |
|---|---|---|
| [[Wisp]] | Str −4 | Almost always fails. 42 HP, so the Whispered Truth plus one Grasp can drop the cat. |
| [[Faerah Duskrane]] | Str −1 | Usually fails |
| [[Redgrave]] | Dex +0 | Usually fails |
| [[Roark Battleborn]] | Int / Wis / Cha +0 | Usually fails |
| [[Yevelda Ovak]] | Int +0 | Usually fails |

Expect the Whispered Truth to land on nearly everyone. That's the point: each PC hears out loud the thing they're weakest at. **Say one true, uncomfortable thing about each target in Vecna's voice** when it lands.

### Izek's tactics, round by round
- **Round 1:** *The Whispered Truth* on everyone. Bonus action: *reset* on the party's fastest PCs (Wis save) to pull their initiative down next round. Legendary: **Grasp** Wisp or Faerah toward the pool. Low Str is what Undertow and Grasp punish.
- **Round 2:**
  - If the party is clustered: *alter time flow*, slowing the party (Con save) and **hasting himself**. That's his best single spell; the barbarians' Con +7 is the only thing that resists it well.
  - If they're spread out: *time slippage* (Int save, stunned) on whichever barbarian is doing the most damage, plus a battleaxe attack.
- **Round 3, the hesitation beat:** the arm flares. Izek raises the black hand at Yevelda, fingers already curling into *finger of death*. Everyone hears him say **"No."** He wrenches the hand aside, the discharge hits the pool and an **Arc-lash** goes off, and he casts *synaptic static* instead. Keep *finger of death* (61 average) in your pocket as the lever: let it land only if the party is cruising, and **never on Wisp** unless you mean to kill the cat.
- **Round 4:** *chaotic world* if his concentration dropped; otherwise multiattack plus **Grasp** into the pool.
- **Reactions:**
  - *counterspell* (3/day) on Wisp's big spells, or on Redgrave's healing when someone's down.
  - *swift exchange* (1/day) when he's attacked: he swaps places with the attacker, ideally leaving the attacker at the pool's edge or in it.
- **Concentration counterplay:** he holds one of *alter time flow*, *time slippage* or *chaotic world* at a time. His Con +11 makes it hard to break by damage alone. The **Hunger** surge is the party's opening, so describe the pool pulling at the spell when it happens.
- **Radiant shuts off his regeneration.** That rewards Redgrave's Sun Blade and the Holy Symbol of Ravenkind. When radiant lands, describe the amber threads going dark for a heartbeat.

### The trigger: Izek at 162 HP or lower
> [!quote] What They See
> He staggers and stops. For a moment he just stands there, breathing, and the arm flares so bright that the green goes white. Then he drives the black hand into the pool up to the elbow. Every line in the dome lights at once. From the Foundry passage comes the sound of iron cradles opening, and something heavy and dark takes its first step. Green smoke begins to pour off Izek, curling upward, as if something is pulling him out of the room by the edges.

- **The Wardens wake.** The [[Amber Warden]] and one [[Unfinished Amber Warden]] tear free of their Foundry cradles and come through on initiative 20 of the next round. **The Amber Warden drags three live feed lines** that still run back into the basin, so **the tether is live** (see [[Greyfen Hollow]] → Feed Lines): it regains 20 HP each turn and its conduits can't drop below 1 HP until **all three lines are cut** (AC 15, 25 HP each; each cut opens a Vent). Show the lines pulsing in time with the Warden's light. A DC 12 Arcana check, or just a player asking about the pipes, explains it. There are no cultists left to re-seat lines.
- **Izek is being recalled.** At the start of his next turn he's gone. Nothing prevents this: it's Vecna pulling him back, not a spell. He can't drop below 1 HP before then and won't act except to *Unwritten Step*.
- **The party will try to stop him. Let it count.** Any creature that hits him, grapples him, or otherwise gets hands on him during the recall round tears loose the **courier case** strapped across his chest (see The Story Find). Narrate the strap snapping. If nobody reaches him, the case is pulled half into the smoke and **falls into the pool**: the waybill is lost (a copy survives in D2), and the seal-master survives in the basin (it isn't magic; see below).
- **His last moment:** he looks at Yevelda, says nothing, and is gone. The smoke hangs where he was for a few seconds, then the pool takes it.

### Phase 2: the Wardens
- **Amber Warden:** goes for whoever is cutting its lines. *Bound Wail* when two or more PCs are within 15 ft.
- **Unfinished Warden:** slow and blunt. It walks straight into the pool if that's the short way, and takes the force damage without caring. When it drops, its **Collapse** goes off, and a filled conduit can be salvaged from the wreck (that's what enables Hijack).
- **Constructs don't break.** The fight ends when they're down.

## After the Fight

### The Story Find: Izek's courier case
A flat case of black leather with iron clasps, stamped with the **courier's sigil**, the same one on every cask along the far wall. Inside are two things.

> [!info] 1. The Waybill: *where next*
> A folded sheet in a clerk's tidy hand. Cask lots are numbered, and each lot is measured in **"charge"**, not gallons. The route is:
> - **Greyfen → [[Duskferry]]:** carried up as "marsh peat, Guild-sealed," re-stamped at the Guildhall Roost *"by the Tallyman's hand."*
> - **Duskferry → onward:** handed to the courier at the Waterline. Destination given only as the courier sigil.
> - **Next collection: twelve casks, due at Duskferry's Waterline at the ninth bell, ten days from tonight.** *(Set the exact fc-date against the calendar.)*
>
> This is the party's next actionable lead. It pays off the [[The Second Ledger|Second Ledger]]'s "Tallyman" thread ([[Ostwin Reyes]], still unmet) and puts a clock on the courier handoff.

> [!info] 2. The Seal-Master: *what it's for*
> A palm-sized plate of black glass with a handle, the master stamp used to press every cask's wax seal. Hold a seal up next to it and they match. It's engraved in a script none of the party reads on sight (**DC 18 Arcana**, *comprehend languages*, or [[Wisp]] with an hour and their spellbook). Translated:
>
> *For the Charging of the Word.*
> *Let every hidden thing be spoken, and every tongue be bound to truth, save the Speaker's.*
>
> Players can work out that the distillate is **fuel for a ritual**, that the ritual **forces truth on everyone**, and that it carves out **one exception**. The cult's Great Unveiling doctrine ("all hidden truths will surface") now has a mechanism and a cost.

> [!warning]- DM-only: what the seal-master really is
> The engraved line is the **exception clause of the World-Truth Working**: the hypocrisy that stakes Vecna's claim to the Truth-domain and makes it contestable at the Rite (see the campaign bible, Endings). The party is seeing the ending's key lever more than an act early. **Don't explain it.** Let them hold it.
> - It tells them nothing about *where* the Working will be cast. That's still The Convergence (Act I, Arc 6).
> - The courier sigil's destination is a dead end until they catch a courier.

> [!info] Using the seal-master: *a key to the cult's doors*
> The stamp has no magic of its own (that's why the pool can't touch it). The Silent Ascendency's wards and gatekeepers are built to recognize its pattern, which is what makes it useful. The party learns this by testing it. Nothing in Greyfen is warded this way, so the first real test is [[Duskferry]]. *(Optional, if you want them to learn it tonight: make the coin in C1 a small sigil-locked strongbox that opens only to a fresh impression. Without the seal it takes DC 18 Thieves' Tools or brute force, and forcing it sets off an alarm glyph.)*
> - **Wards:** cult glyph-locks, alarm wards, and sealed doors keyed to the courier sigil treat a fresh wax impression, or the stamp itself pressed to the glyph, as authorization. The bearer and anyone walking with them pass without triggering it. This does nothing against wards the cult didn't make.
> - **Papers:** pressing a seal takes 1 minute and some wax. Orders, manifests, or letters stamped with it pass rank-and-file cultists without a check. Anyone posing as a courier or cask handler has **advantage on Deception** checks to keep the cover going.
> - **Not against officers:** the Tallyman, cell leaders, and anyone who handles waybills knows which seal goes with which shipment. Using it in front of them gives advantage on *their* Insight checks to spot a forgery.

> [!warning]- DM-only: how long the seal stays good
> It depends on how the party got it:
> - **Torn off Izek during the recall:** Izek knows it's gone, and the Voice knows what Izek knows. It works cleanly through [[Duskferry]]. After that, each lair they use it on has a 1-in-6 chance (rising by 1 per use) that the cult has re-keyed its wards there, and that ward goes off instead.
> - **Fished out of the pool:** Izek believes it was destroyed. No re-keying until the party gives themselves away, for example by being seen using it, or by someone escaping who saw them use it. Then switch to the clock above.
> - The cult re-keying is a story beat, not just a penalty. A new seal pattern in circulation means somebody is carving masters. That is a lead for later.

**Three clues that the distillate charges a ritual:** the seal-master's "For the Charging of the Word"; the waybill measuring casks in "charge"; and the Sorting Floor's intake ledger, whose second column records charge yielded, in C1 on the way out.

### D2: Izek's Watch
Run it as written in [[Greyfen Hollow]] and the 2026-10-17 prep: the cold cot, the whetstone, the Vallakian wine gone to vinegar, the shelf of clumsy carvings. **One carving is unmistakably Yevelda.** Describe it once, plainly, and let the table sit in it. Also here:
- **The working copy of the waybill**, in Izek's own blocky hand (in case the case went into the pool).
- **A greataxe** propped beside the shelf. It's too fine for him and the wrong weapon for him. See treasure.

### D3: the Cold Room
DC 17 Investigation behind the north wall: the one dry wall in a weeping cave, with wear-polish where the slab sweeps. Dozens of filled conduits racked like wine bottles, warm, holding *people*. Breaking one frees a soul. That's worth narrating, not worth anything on a character sheet.

### Out
Same two choices as the 2026-10-17 prep:
- **Burn it down:** break three glyph-stones and run. The lift ride up has ten rounds on the clock.
- **Leave with the evidence:** casks, tallies, the intake ledger, the case.

Either way, they come up into the dead trees knowing that Vecna talks to Izek through his own severed hand, that the magic is being melted down to charge a ritual that forces truth on the world, and that a courier is due in Duskferry in ten days.

## Secrets and Clues
> [!tip] 10 Secrets
> 1. Izek's left arm ends in **the Hand of Vecna**. Stories say only a hand and an eye survive of the Whispered One's body.
> 2. Vecna **speaks to Izek through the Hand**. Izek isn't possessed: he answers and he obeys.
> 3. Izek **doesn't want to kill them**. He turned the killing spell aside from Yevelda and said "No" out loud.
> 4. Izek's amber veins match the Wardens' conduits and [[Marrow]]'s graft. He's the original the Wardens imitate.
> 5. The distillate is **fuel for a ritual**: "For the Charging of the Word."
> 6. The ritual **binds every tongue to truth, save the Speaker's.**
> 7. Casks leave through [[Duskferry]] under Guild seal, re-stamped by **"the Tallyman."**
> 8. A courier collects **twelve casks at Duskferry in ten days.**
> 9. About **a third** of everything distilled here leaves in casks (the D1 draw tallies).
> 10. Izek has been **carving Yevelda** in his Watch.

## Important NPCs
> [!info] Izek Strazni: silent, huge, sorry
> **Voice/mannerism:** one-word or one-line answers; long pauses while he listens to the Hand; looks at Yevelda too long. Never gloats, never explains.
> **If the party talks instead of fighting:** "Leave." "Go home." Asked about Vecna: he looks at the hand. Asked what the casks are for: silence. Asked about Yevelda: he looks at her and doesn't answer, and that *is* the answer.
> **DM-only:** lucid, not compelled, paying a debt. He'd rather they lived. Full detail in [[Izek Strazni (COS)]].

> [!info] The Voice in the Hand (Vecna, off-screen)
> **Never heard directly by the party.** Only Izek's half of the conversation is audible. If anyone uses magic to listen in (*detect thoughts* on Izek, for example), they catch a single phrase in a dry, patient, amused voice: *"Then prove it."* Nothing more.

## DM-Only: Contingencies
> [!warning]- Read before the table, not at it
> - **If they actually leave when told:** Izek lets them go. The Wardens still wake behind them as the pool surges, and the fight happens at the lift, with no Izek and no case. The D2 waybill copy becomes the only clue, so make sure they get a reason to go back for it, or move the waybill copy to a cultist corpse at the lift.
> - **If they try to talk him down:** play the one-sided conversation anyway. Vecna's answer to anything they say is the order. Persuasion can win one thing: a **DC 20** check gets one honest sentence before the fight, *"He gave me back my arm when nobody else would. I owe him."* It doesn't stop the fight.
> - **If Wisp is targeted by *finger of death*:** that's a kill unless the party has Revivify ready. Don't do it by accident.
> - **If they break three glyph-stones mid-fight:** the ten-round clock starts, and Phase 2 becomes a running fight to the lift. The Wardens follow. The courier case must be in hand or it's gone.
> - **If someone goes down in the pool:** they keep taking 4d10 force each turn they start there, plus death-save failures. Make the rescue the drama.
> - **If they get him to 162 HP in round 1** (unlikely, but possible with a nova): he still gets the "Not her" beat. Run it as he raises the hand to recall, not as a combat round.
> - **If they try to follow him:** the recall leaves nothing to follow. *Detect magic* or similar shows the smoke going **down**, into the pool. A true dead end.
> - **After the session:**
>   - Flip Izek's `partyRelationships` off *Friendly*. "Enemy" may still be the wrong word.
>   - Update [[The Second Ledger]] steps and its Duskferry courier lead.
