## Strong Start — The Lift Comes Back Up

> [!quote] What They See
> The last guard hits the grating and the cave takes the sound and keeps it — passes it from wall to wall, down and away, until it's just another drip among thousands. Then the quiet comes back, and in it, something new: the elevator gate behind you rattles on its runners, chains begin to pay out overhead with a slow brass *tick-tick-tick* of counterweights, and the empty cage starts rising back up the shaft on its own.
>
> Somebody, somewhere, pulled a lever. The cage is going up to fetch whoever's next.

**What it actually is:** the lift runs on a fixed schedule — a supply run, a load of crates from the cap coming down for the Sorting Floor. It's **not** a response to the fight. But the party doesn't know that, and the cage will come back down in **about 5 minutes** with **2 [[5. Mechanics/Bestiary/Humanoid/Cultist.md|Cultists]]** and a pallet of crated stolen goods.

That gives them one good, tense problem to open on: three prisoners, a mess of bodies, blood on the grating, and a lift on its way down.
- **Hide the scene and ambush the lift** — easy win, two more prisoners or bodies, and the crates are a loot preview (Tier 1 clue: one is tagged by weight in "charge," not price).
- **Bluff the lift crew** — DC 13 Deception (wearing guard kit/tabards gives advantage). They want help unloading, nothing more.
- **Get out of sight and let it pass** — the crew unloads and wheels the pallet toward the Gallery, and the party can tail them straight to the Sorting Floor.

None of these raise the alarm unless someone gets loud. A sloppy result moves the ladder to **1 — Uneasy**, not 2.

> [!tip] The real clock (DM pacing tool)
> The guards they beat are due to be **relieved at shift change in roughly two hours**. When the relief finds an empty post, the ladder jumps straight to **2 — Sweep**. This is the background pressure all session. It's also what a **short rest costs**: one hour of a two-hour margin.

## Scene 1 — The Prisoners

*Depends on: [[Greyfen Hollow]] (Hidden Details → Tier 1). Social encounter, fast.*

Three captured cult members, freshly beaten and frightened, but not broken — they believe in this.

> [!info] The three (voices for the table)
> - **The zealot:** young, smiling through a split lip, keeps saying "the Great Unveiling." Volunteers the Tier 1 truth *proudly* without being pressed: "We are not thieves. We are *unmaking* the lie of them. Every enchantment is a secret someone kept. We give the secrets back to Him." Never says "Vecna." Says "the Whispered One."
> - **The hireling:** a paid guard, not a believer. Older, scarred, practical. Cooperates for his life with a **DC 12 Persuasion/Intimidation**. Knows the patrol loop (two-person, every ten minutes through the Gallery), the shift change, the Cistern's two veterans "who actually get paid," and the **crack behind the flowstone column** that the cook uses to smuggle wine in from the Sunken Passage. *(This is the B4 route handed over for free, the reward for taking prisoners.)*
> - **The quiet one:** says nothing at all unless someone gets a **DC 15 Insight** read on her: she's terrified, but not of the party. Pressed gently (DC 14 Persuasion), she whispers about "the big man in the Watch" — the one who never sleeps, sits above the Reservoir, and "already knows you're here. He always knows." She will not describe him further; she's seen what his arm does.

**Do not let them name Izek.** They don't know his name. They know a huge, scarred, silent overseer with one wrong arm. If Yevelda's player puts it together from that, let them. It's dread, not confirmation.

**Prisoner logistics are the party's problem.** Gagging them, tying them in the lift cage, leaving a guard, or worse — all valid, and all with costs (a guard left behind is one less PC; a cage of prisoners is found at shift change). Let the party choose; the bible's grit line means "kill the bound prisoners" is a choice with weight, not a free action — narrate it plainly if they make it.

## Scene 2 — The Weeping Gallery (B2)

*Depends on: [[Greyfen Hollow]] → B2, B3, B4.*

> [!quote] What They See
> The passage opens and the ceiling simply *leaves*. Mineral curtains hang from the dark in folded sheets, translucent as candle-wax, banded rust and cream and a deep amber where the cult's conduit lines run behind them and light them from inside like lanterns hung in flesh. Water beads on every surface and falls — not in drips but in a soft, endless, overlapping patter, as if the whole chamber were whispering to itself and stopping each time you try to hear a word. Underfoot the flowstone is ridged like frozen ripples. Far off, through the curtains, something enormous and mechanical breathes: a slow *hiss* — *clank* — *hiss*, the rhythm of the deep works.

- **Mechanics (from the location note):** advantage on Stealth, disadvantage on hearing-Perception, for both sides. Patrol every 10 minutes.
- **Two ways on:** the worked passage to **B3 The Cistern** (six off-shift cultists, two veterans, the alarm bell) or the crack to **B4 The Sunken Passage** (DC 14 Investigation / DC 12 Perception to find, or automatic if the hireling talked).
- **Cistern fight if stealth collapses:** 6 [[Cultist]] + 2 [[Veteran]] = 6.75 CR, a survivable brawl in a bad room. The real cost is the bell: **ladder to 2**.

**Short rest window:** the far end of B4, past the squeeze, is a dead-end side pocket nobody patrols — cold, wet, dark, and genuinely safe for an hour. If the party asks, it's there. It costs half their shift-change margin.

## Scene 3 — The Sorting Floor (C1)

*Depends on: [[Greyfen Hollow]] → C1. The loot room. 4 Cultists + 1 Cult Fanatic overseer on shift.*

> [!quote] What They See
> Straw on stone, and crates stacked to the ceiling in rows like a merchant's warehouse — except every one of them hums. Stand still and you can feel it in your teeth: a low, mixed chord of a hundred enchantments packed close together, none of them quite in tune with the others. Lantern light falls on a clerk's high desk with a ledger chained to it, and on open racks where things that should be treasured lie in straw like turnips: a circlet with one stone missing, a sheathed sword that steams faintly in the cold, a child's music box that plays three notes and stops, plays three notes and stops.

- **Tier 1 clue (the intake ledger):** items in by name; a second column, in a different hand, records *charge yielded* by weight. Nothing is resold.
- **Fight if it comes to it:** 4 [[Cultist]] + 1 [[Cult Fanatic]] = 2.5 CR, a speed bump. The overseer's first instinct is to run for the Distillery and warn the floor (**ladder to 1**, or **2** if he makes it).
- **Treasure:** see Potential Treasure below — this is the main haul.

## Scene 4 — The Distillery (C2)

*Depends on: [[Greyfen Hollow]] → C2, Machinery (Extraction Cradles, Raw Magic Vents). 4 Cultists + 1 Cult Fanatic.*

> [!quote] What They See
> Noise first — a wall of it. Three long rows of iron cradles, each one clamping a single magic item in a frame veined with amber, and every frame *working*: humming, ticking, groaning in its own key. In the nearest, a wand is going grey from the tip down, the color draining out of it like ink from a pen, and as the grey reaches each carved rune the rune gutters, flares once — bright, almost pleading — and goes out. Thin amber liquid beads on the frame beneath it and runs off through copper pipework into the floor. Above it all, two tiers of iron catwalks cross the room in the steam, and up there the lanterns are hung so that anyone on the floor is lit and anyone above is not.

- **This is the room that teaches the machinery before the boss fight.** Thirty seconds of watching a cradle is a Tier 1 clue. Let a curious PC Salvage (DC 15 Arcana or thieves' tools) and see what failure-by-5 does — the Vent that opens here is the same thing they'll be juggling in the Foundry.
- **Two cradles hold items mid-unmaking** (salvageable; see treasure). Every round of combat here, each un-salvaged item has a 1-in-6 chance of finishing and dissolving.
- **Catwalks:** the whole room can be crossed overhead. Sightlines are the cult's defense, not the noise.

## Scene 5 — The Reservoir, Izek's Watch, and the Cold Room (D1–D3)

*Depends on: [[Greyfen Hollow]] → D1, D2, D3, Hidden Details → Tier 2.*

> [!quote] What They See — The Reservoir
> A dome of raw rock, and in its floor a basin of still amber light — a shallow lake of something that isn't water, glowing from underneath, throwing slow, rippling shadows up the walls so the whole chamber seems to be moving while it stands still. It has no reflection. Look into it and you don't see yourself; you see depth, and warmth, and, for half a heartbeat, the suggestion of faces pressing up from below like people under ice. Six standing glyph-stones ring the basin and hum on a note just below hearing. Your fillings ache. Along the far wall, waxed casks are stacked on pallets, each stamped with the same small courier's sigil — a sigil that is not on anything else in this cave.

- **Tier 2 clues:** the draw tallies (a third of production leaves in casks), the courier sigil on casks vs. Foundry marks elsewhere, and — if a Fanatic is ever taken alive and pressed — the one who was told not to count, and did.
- **Hold the line on what the casks are for.** Nobody here knows except Izek, and he isn't saying.

> [!quote] What They See — Izek's Watch (D2)
> A ledge above the basin, cut back into the rock. A cot nobody has slept in. A whetstone. A bottle of red wine, uncorked, long gone to vinegar — the label is Vallakian. A battleaxe leaning exactly where a very large man could reach it without standing up. And a shelf of small wooden carvings, clumsy and gouged, made by a hand that was not built for fine work and kept trying anyway.

- **The carvings (kept, per DM):** most are shapeless lumps. One isn't — a tall, broad-shouldered woman with a greatsword across her back. It is unmistakably [[Yevelda Ovak]]. Describe it plainly, once, and let the table sit in it. Don't explain it. The Vallakian wine and the Blinsky echo are there for anyone who remembers S9.
- **No one is here.** The cot is cold. He's watching from somewhere else.

**D3 — The Cold Room:** DC 17 Investigation behind the north wall (the tell: the one dry wall in a weeping cave, wear-polish where the slab sweeps). Dozens of filled conduits racked like wine bottles. Warm. Audible if you hold still: not words, just *people*, very tired. The party may want to free them — each one broken releases a soul (worth narrating, worth nothing on a sheet). A filled conduit is also the key to **Hijacking** an Unfinished Warden in the Foundry; let them make that connection themselves.

## Scene 6 — The Foundry: Izek's Exit (C3)

*Depends on: [[Greyfen Hollow]] → C3, Machinery (Feed Lines, Warden Cradles), Encounters; [[Izek Strazni (COS)]] → Hidden Details. **This is the first Amber Warden the party has ever seen.***

**Trigger:** the party enters C3 or D1, the alarm hits 3, or the DM decides it's time. Izek's call, not the party's failure — a perfect infiltration still gets this scene.

> [!quote] What They See — The Foundry
> Iron cradles stand in ranks like gallows, and in them hang the bodies of giants — or the beginnings of them. Stone legs with no torso. A spine of fitted grey blocks with ribs half-set. Two that are nearly whole, chests open, waiting for hearts. And at the room's far end, standing in its cradle with three thick amber lines plugged into its shoulders and chest like a patient on a surgeon's table, one that is finished. It is dark. It has always been dark. Then the amber in it *breathes* — three slow points of light, swelling and fading on a rhythm that isn't quite a heartbeat — and from somewhere deep inside the stone comes a sound you will remember for the rest of your life: a thin chorus of overlapping voices, wordless, and so, so tired.

> [!quote] What They See — Izek
> He's on the upper catwalk. You don't know how long he's been there. He is bigger than you remember, and he was big then. The arm is worse — swollen, ridged, the fiend-flesh grown up past the shoulder and into the side of his neck — and through all of it, thin as thread, run the same veins of amber light that are pulsing in the thing in the cradle below.
>
> He looks at you. All of you. And then at Yevelda, for a beat too long.

> [!quote] Izek Strazni
> "You shouldn't have come down here."
>
> *(a pause, as if he's deciding whether to say anything else, and doesn't)*
>
> "Wake it."

Then he turns his back and walks out through the Reservoir. **He does not fight.** If anyone attacks or gives chase, run it per his note: one round to show the numbers, then Whispered Step and gone. The last thing they see is that he looks sorry, and that it didn't change anything.

### The fight

```encounter
name: The Foundry — Izek's Exit (default, no rest)
creatures:
  - 1: [[Amber Warden]]
  - 1: [[Unfinished Amber Warden]]
  - 2: [[Cult Fanatic]]
  - 1: [[Veteran]]
  - 4: [[Cultist]]
---
name: The Foundry — Izek's Exit (full, after a short rest)
creatures:
  - 1: [[Amber Warden]]
  - 2: [[Unfinished Amber Warden]]
  - 2: [[Cult Fanatic]]
  - 1: [[Veteran]]
  - 4: [[Cultist]]
```

> **CR math (Lazy DM benchmark).** 5 PCs × level 10 = 50 summed levels; potentially deadly above summed CR **25**.
> - **Default (no rest): 22.5** — [[Amber Warden]] 10 + one [[Unfinished Amber Warden]] 5 + 2× [[Cult Fanatic]] 4 + [[Veteran]] 3 + 4× [[Cultist]] 0.5. Just under the deadly line on paper, but the tether makes it play harder than the number. **This is the recommended start**, since the party hasn't rested since S47.
> - **Full (if they short-rested in B4): 27.5** — as built in [[Greyfen Hollow]], above the deadly line. Treat a PC going down as expected.
> - **Dials mid-fight:** going badly → cultists panic instead of re-seating lines. Going too well → a Fanatic releases the second Unfinished Warden (+5) or deliberately overloads a cradle to seed Vents.
>
> **Start state — the tether is live.** All three feed lines are seated at round 1. The Warden regains 20 HP at the start of its turns and its conduits can't drop below 1 HP until **all three lines are cut** (AC 15, 25 HP each; a cut opens a Vent; a cultist adjacent to a cut line can re-seat it as an action). Give the tell early: the lines visibly pulse in time with the Warden's light. DC 12 Arcana/Investigation, or just "what are those pipes doing," spells it out.
>
> **Terrain, tactics, morale:** per [[Greyfen Hollow]] → Encounters. Two catwalk levels (15-ft drop), cradles as cover and as loaded guns, Vents accumulating. Fanatics think (re-seat lines, release cradles, *hold person* on whoever's at the pipework); Veteran covers them; Cultists pull levers and break when half are down. Constructs never break.
>
> **Hijack is live** if they brought a filled conduit from D3: DC 17 Arcana, an Unfinished Warden fights for them for 1 minute, then turns on everyone.

## Scene 7 — Out

*Depends on: [[Greyfen Hollow]] → Containment Ring.*

Two real choices, both legitimate strategic wins:
- **Burn it down.** Destroy three of the six glyph-stones (AC 17, 30 HP each): ten rounds later the deep level goes up (10d6 force, DC 18 Dex half, in C2/C3/D1) and the caves come down behind them. Destroys the stockpile, a real dent in the Ascendency's timeline — and destroys the evidence, the un-looted C1 items, and every filled conduit in D3 (which frees them, if someone frames it that way). Ten rounds to the gate elevator is tight but doable, and the lift is slow. Make that ride up *agonizing*: brass ticking, counterweights, the cage rising while the light below goes from amber to white.
- **Leave with the evidence.** Casks, tallies, the intake ledger, a filled conduit. Proof to show someone — and the works keep running.

Either way, they come up into the dead trees knowing three things they didn't this morning: the Ascendency is unmaking magic at scale; a third of it is going somewhere else; and Izek Strazni is alive, lucid, and chose this.

**Natural stopping points if time runs short:** after the Sorting Floor (loot in hand, clock ticking), or **on Izek's "Wake it."** — the strongest cliffhanger the site has.

## Secrets and Clues

> [!tip] 10 Secrets
> 1. The magic items are being **unmade** into raw magic, not resold — the intake ledger, watching a cradle, or any cultist proves it.
> 2. The rank-and-file think it's holy work for "the Whispered One" and the Great Unveiling; none of them know who sits above the cell.
> 3. About a **third** of the distillate leaves in sealed casks carrying a courier's sigil, not a Foundry mark.
> 4. One Cult Fanatic was told explicitly not to count the casks — and has counted them, for months.
> 5. This site **builds Amber Wardens** at every stage of assembly; the finished one in the Foundry is the first, not the last.
> 6. Live feed lines from the Reservoir make the Foundry Warden effectively unkillable — cut all three.
> 7. Filled conduits hold **people**. Breaking one frees whoever is inside — and holding one lets you hijack an unfinished Warden.
> 8. The ancient cap was built to **seal** something in, and the Ascendency cut it open.
> 9. Izek's grafted limbs carry the same amber veining as the Warden conduits and Marrow's graft — by sight, no roll.
> 10. Izek knew the party was in the caves the whole time and let them come down. "The big man in the Watch always knows."

## DM-Only — Contingencies

> [!warning]- Read before the table, not at it
> - **If the party skips straight to C3/D1:** Izek triggers immediately. Skipping the Sorting Floor costs the loot and the Brooch's warning; don't backfill it.
> - **If the shift-change clock runs out:** ladder to 2 — Sweep. The Cistern bell rings, off-shift cultists arm up, and Izek is watching closer. Push toward a forced Foundry scene rather than a cave-wide grind.
> - **If someone attacks Izek:** one round, numbers on the table, then Whispered Step. If they corner him anyway, he fights in earnest — make sure it's clear they chose a CR 17 solo before the dice.
> - **If the party tries to talk to Izek:** he gives them almost nothing. "You shouldn't have come." "Go home." Asked about Vecna: silence. Asked about the casks: silence. Asked about Yevelda: he looks at her and doesn't answer, and that *is* the answer.
> - **If they push for where the casks go:** a true dead end at this depth. The Convergence (Act I, Arc 6) owns that answer.
> - **If they burn it down with the Cold Room unbroken:** every filled conduit shatters in the blast. The souls are freed — let someone hear the chorus stop. That's a mercy *and* a loss of evidence.
> - **If a PC goes down in the Foundry:** expected, not a failure state. The Periapt of Wound Closure is right there in C1 for a reason.
> - **After the session:** flip Izek's `partyRelationships` off *Friendly* (the DM's word — "Enemy" may not be right), and the bible's Act II Arc 3 revision is now due.
