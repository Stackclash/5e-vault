> |---|---|
> | **Bright Light** | Normal vision |
> | **Dim Light** | Lightly obscured; disadvantage on Perception (sight) |
> | **Darkness** | Heavily obscured; effectively blinded |
> | **Darkvision** | Dim light → bright; darkness → dim (no color) |
> | **Lightly Obscured** | Disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) relying on sight |

> [!abstract]- Concentration
> A caster maintaining concentration must make a CON save when:
> - Taking damage (DC = 10 or half damage taken, whichever is higher)
> - Incapacitated or killed
> - Environmental effects (DM discretion, DC 10)
>
> Only **one** concentration spell at a time.
