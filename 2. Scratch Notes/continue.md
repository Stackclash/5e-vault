## Locked structural decisions

**Two Acts:**
- **Act I** climaxes with **Ritual 1 — The World-Truth Working**. First direct fight with Vecna, and the party **loses** — overwhelmed and beaten, but alive. Vecna is functionally unstoppable while completing the casting (deliberate — a straight fight isn't the win condition here).
  - Survival mechanism for that loss is **still deliberately undecided** — keep three options live and pick whichever lands hardest at the table: (1) Vecna's contempt (he's spent, doesn't bother finishing beneath-notice mortals); (2) ritual backlash (the Working's completion knocks them out/scatters them, possibly with a lingering side-effect echoing Thornmere's "too much truth" overflow); (3) a rescuer pulls them out at a cost, creating a debt. **Do not pre-select.**
- **Act II** opens **low-stakes**: aftermath of the Working, exploring how the world changed, side-story/personal-arc room. Climaxes with **Ritual 2 — The Ascension Rite** — the true, decisive final battle, fate of the world in the balance.

## Vecna's plan, corrected (supersedes the campaign bible's current text — bible still needs this edit made)

- **Thornmere was only a small-scale prototype** — proof the truth-magic works at village scale. The bible currently says "Force Truth" is complete; that's wrong and needs fixing when we file changes.
- **Vecna's real goal:** force truth onto most/all of the world — a working at civilizational scale. That scale/permanence elevates Truth from "a thing that happens" into a **metaphysical constant**, a cosmic domain-slot on par with Death or Time. Reality "needs" a God of Truth once that domain exists.
- **The trap in his own spell:** it compels everyone *else* to truth while Vecna alone can define/manipulate what "true" means. That asymmetry is the actual goal — **and it's also the mechanism of his claim on the domain** (see Endings below). It's his hypocrisy, not his power, that's the exploitable weakness.
- **Two separate rituals:**
  1. **The World-Truth Working** (Act I climax) — forces the global compulsion, creates the vacant Truth-domain, **and stakes Vecna's claim to it** via that exception-to-his-own-law trick.
  2. **The Ascension Rite** (Act II climax) — Vecna formally executes/validates that claim and becomes the God of Truth. Campaign ends here.
- Both rituals have **multiple setup stages the party can witness, piece together, and potentially disrupt** — consequences if they fail, a Vecna workaround (not a hard wall) if they succeed at a given stage.

## Magic-item power pipeline (agreed, unchanged)

- Smuggled magic items (Velmont's ring, Umbral Athenaeum) are drained into constructed **soul-vessels** built from stolen fragments of **Eludrax's** forbidden soul-craft — the same craft that (unknown to Strahd) fed the original seal binding Vecna at the Amber Temple, and the same craft that produced [[Redgrave]]'s bound soul. Closed-loop irony, and it's why the Order of the Silver Dragon would take notice (they can detect the signature) — reuses [[Redgrave]]'s existing Character Tie-In rather than inventing a new thread.
- **Doppelgangers are NOT thematically tied to "stolen truth"** — retracted early idea, unsupported leap. They're just practical Silent Ascendency infiltration tradecraft (per S43).
- **Recurring monster motif (DM's idea, strong, keep):** some soul-vessels are grown into large mechanical guardian constructs — amber conduits threaded through the frame, carrying bound souls of cult victims as a power source. Working name floated: **Amber Wardens** (not DM-confirmed yet). Recurs across Act I (and likely beyond) guarding anchors/vessel caches — echoes the Stone Golem fight from S43, pulls Amber Temple imagery through the whole hunt. **No stat block work done** — `statblock-creator`/`encounter-builder` job when a concrete encounter needs one.

## The finale: how the vacancy gets filled, and the alternate endings (confirmed mechanism)

- **Ritual 1 stakes Vecna's claim on the vacancy** — his exception-to-universal-truth trick reads to the cosmos as the strongest available claim, because no one else has as total a relationship to the domain (even though his relationship is a rigged cheat, not honest truthfulness).
- **Ritual 2 is where that claim gets formally executed/validated.**
- **The claim is contestable because it isn't honestly won.** A rival claimant with a genuinely total, unhedged (non-exempt) relationship to Truth could override Vecna's reservation at the Rite.
- **Act I's outcome feeds directly into which endings are realistically available:** a patchy/disrupted Working (party did real damage in Act I) leaves Vecna's claim shakier — redirect or collapse become live options. A clean, near-total Working leaves it nearly airtight — "stop him outright" may be the only realistic path, or collapse becomes far costlier.
- **Three endings, DM-confirmed to keep all as possibilities:**
  1. **Stop Vecna outright** — kill/banish him before the Rite completes. Vacancy dissolves unclaimed. Always available regardless of prep — the baseline win.
  2. **Redirect the vacancy to a worthier claimant** — floated candidate: **Wisp's master**, if the Eternal Watch turn out to have groomed a contingency candidate for exactly this scenario. **Gated behind Act II prep** — keeping him alive/stable and completing whatever the Eternal Watch arc requires. DM confirmed keeping this as a possibility but flagged that it depends on the claim-mechanism above being solid — **now resolved, so this ending is back on the table,** not blocked.
  3. **Collapse the domain entirely** — destroy the vacancy so nothing can ever fill it. Cost: Truth-magic (Zone of Truth, Discern Lies, binding oaths, etc.) becomes permanently unreliable in the world. Requires specific knowledge from the Eternal Watch to even be an option.
