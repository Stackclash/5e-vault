## Die Silently
All death saving throws are rolled by the DM, hidden from the players.

## You’re Coming With Me
If you drop to 0 HP you get one reaction for a melee critical hit, but you start your death saving throws with 2 failures.

## Tired After Dying
After falling to 0 HP and being revived, each failed death saving throw is a point of exhaustion.

## Healing Potions
If you use your action to drink a healing potion, the dice roll is maxed, but if you use a bonus action, you have to roll like normal.

## Material Components
Material Components are not necessary for spells unless they are consumed.

## True Critical
When calculating damage for critical hits, max the extra damage dice.

## Usage Die
When dealing with resources that are consumable (ie. ammo, rations, etc) the amount available is represented by a usage die. A usage die can be any of the 6 dice (d20, d12, d10, d8, d6, d4). Anytime an event happens where a resource is used (ie. combat, long rest, etc) the usage dice is rolled. On a 1 or a 2 the dice is downgraded to the next smallest dice.

## Help Me Proficiently
If a player initiates a skills check, another player may also attempt the skill check, but only if they have proficiency in the skill.

## Opportunity Grapple
A player may use grapple as their opportunity attack.

## Where You At
When attacking a creature you cannot see, a player can roll a perception check to see if they can detect where the creature is. The DM will give an area based on the roll where the creature is, and the player can select one square in the area to target. If the player chooses the creature's location, then they roll for the attack with disadvantage as normal.
- 0-5: No area given
- 6-10: 3x3 square
- 11-15: 2x2 square
- 16+: exact location
