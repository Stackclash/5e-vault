# Extra Templates

Additional templates for miscellaneous vault features. Located in `z_Templates/Templater/Extra/` and `z_Templates/Templater/Editing/`.

## Sound Board

**File:** `z_Templates/Templater/Extra/Sound Board.md`

Creates an interactive sound board note for playing background music and ambient sounds during D&D sessions. Works with the [Media Extended](https://github.com/aidenlx/media-extended) plugin to play audio files stored in `z_Assets/`.

## Editing Templates

Located in `z_Templates/Templater/Editing/`:

### Blank Callout

**File:** `z_Templates/Templater/Editing/Blank Callout.md`

Inserts a blank Obsidian callout block for quick formatting.

### Columns

**File:** `z_Templates/Templater/Editing/Columns.md`

Inserts a multi-column layout structure for organizing content side-by-side.
