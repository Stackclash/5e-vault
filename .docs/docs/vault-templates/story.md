# Story Templates

Templates for story and quest management. Located in `z_Templates/Templater/Story/` and `z_Templates/Templater/Campaign/`.

## Quest

**File:** `z_Templates/Templater/Story/Quest.md`

Creates a quest tracking note. The template collects:

- **Quest name** — title of the quest
- **Quest giver** — NPC who assigned the quest
- **Related locations** — where the quest takes place

Quest notes include frontmatter for tracking quest status, objectives, and rewards. They integrate with the party dashboard and session prep templates for cross-referencing.

## Campaign

**File:** `z_Templates/Templater/Campaign/Campaign.md`

Creates a campaign-level note for organizing an entire campaign arc, linking to parties, quests, and world elements.
