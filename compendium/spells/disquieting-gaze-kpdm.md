# Disquieting Gaze
*1st-level, Necromancy*  

- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 minute

Your eyes burn with scintillating motes of unholy crimson light. Until the spell ends, you have advantage on Charisma ([Intimidation](/compendium/rules/skills.md#Intimidation)) checks made against creatures that can see you, and you have advantage on spell attack rolls that deal necrotic damage to creatures that can see your eyes.
