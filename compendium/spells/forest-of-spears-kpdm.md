# Forest of Spears
*5th-level, Evocation*  

- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a sliver of stone)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

A forest of stone spears rises up from the ground in a 30-foot radius around a point you designate within range. Creatures in the area take `dice:5d8|noform|noparens|avg` (`5d8`) piercing damage and are [restrained](/compendium/rules/conditions.md#Restrained). A successful Dexterity saving throw reduces the damage by half and negates the [restrained](/compendium/rules/conditions.md#Restrained) condition. A creature that starts its turn in the area and is already [restrained](/compendium/rules/conditions.md#Restrained) by the spears takes an extra `dice:2d8|noform|noparens|avg` (`2d8`) damage from the pain of being held aloft as well as bleeding from its wounds.

A creature [restrained](/compendium/rules/conditions.md#Restrained) by the spears can use its action to make a Strength or Dexterity check (its choice) against your spell save DC. On a successful save, it frees itself.

For the duration of the spell, the area is difficult terrain. The spears (AC 14) can be damaged; they are immune to piercing damage and have resistance to bludgeoning and slashing damage from nonmagical attacks. If a 5-foot square section of spears takes 15 damage, that's enough to free a [restrained](/compendium/rules/conditions.md#Restrained) creature or clear the section of spears.
