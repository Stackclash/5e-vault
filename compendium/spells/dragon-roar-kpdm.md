# Dragon Roar
*cantrip, Evocation*  

- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

Your voice is amplified to assault the mind of one creature. The target must make a Charisma saving throw. If it fails, the target takes `dice:1d4|noform|noparens|avg` (`d4`) psychic damage and is [frightened](/compendium/rules/conditions.md#Frightened) until the start of your next turn. A target can be affected by your *dragon roar* only once per 24 hours.

This spell's damage increases by `dice:1d4|noform|noparens|avg` (`d4`) when you reach 5th level (`dice:2d4|noform|noparens|avg` (`2d4`)), 11th level (`dice:3d4|noform|noparens|avg` (`3d4`)), and 17th level (`dice:4d4|noform|noparens|avg` (`4d4`)).
