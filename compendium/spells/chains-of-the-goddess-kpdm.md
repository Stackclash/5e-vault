# Chains of the Goddess
*5th-level, Enchantment*  

- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 90 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (1 foot of iron chain)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Choose a creature you can see within 90 feet. The target must make a successful Wisdom saving throw or be [restrained](/compendium/rules/conditions.md#Restrained) by chains of psychic force and take `dice:6d8|noform|noparens|avg` (`6d8`) bludgeoning damage. A [restrained](/compendium/rules/conditions.md#Restrained) creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a successful save. While [restrained](/compendium/rules/conditions.md#Restrained) in this way, the creature also takes `dice:6d8|noform|noparens|avg` (`6d8`) bludgeoning damage at the start of each of your turns.

**Classes**: [Cleric](/compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-cleric.md); [Bard](/compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md)

*Source: Deep Magic p. 48*
