# Harsh Light of Summer's Glare
*8th-level, Enchantment*  

- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 90 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 round

You emit a burst of brilliant light, which bears down oppressively upon all creatures within range that can see you. Creatures with [darkvision](/compendium/rules/senses.md#Darkvision) that fail a Constitution saving throw are [blinded](/compendium/rules/conditions.md#Blinded) and [stunned](/compendium/rules/conditions.md#Stunned). Creatures without [darkvision](/compendium/rules/senses.md#Darkvision) that fail a Constitution saving throw are [blinded](/compendium/rules/conditions.md#Blinded). This is not a gaze attack, and it cannot be avoided by averting one's eyes or wearing a blindfold.

**Classes**: [Druid](/compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-druid.md); [Sorcerer](/compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-sorcerer.md); [Wizard](/compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-wizard.md); [Bard](/compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md)
