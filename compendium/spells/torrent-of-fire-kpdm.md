# Torrent of Fire
*4th-level, Conjuration*  

- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (60-foot Cone)
- **Components:** V, S, M (a piece of obsidian)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You harness the power of fire contained in ley lines with this spell. You create a 60-foot cone of flame. Creatures in the cone take `dice:6d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`6d6`) fire damage, or half as much damage with a successful Dexterity saving throw. You can then flow along the flames, reappearing anywhere inside the cone's area. This repositioning doesn't count as movement and doesn't trigger [opportunity attacks](/compendium/rules/actions.md#Opportunity%20Attack).

**Classes**: [Sorcerer](/compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-sorcerer.md); [Bard](/compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md)
