# Ravages of Time
*7th-level, Transmutation*  

- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a wilted flower)
- **Duration:** 24 hours

You accelerate the aging of one creature within range, causing it to take on the state of a decrepit version of its type. The target suffers three levels of [exhaustion](/compendium/rules/conditions.md#Exhaustion) that cannot be removed by normal means. A [greater restoration](/compendium/spells/greater-restoration.md) spell partially reverses the effect of this spell, removing one level of [exhaustion](/compendium/rules/conditions.md#Exhaustion). Constructs and undead are unaffected by this spell, and creatures who live to great age without negative effects, such as dragons, are also unaffected.

**Classes**: [Sorcerer](/compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-sorcerer.md); [Wizard](/compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-wizard.md); [Cleric](/compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-cleric.md); [Bard](/compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md)

*Source: Deep Magic p. 259*
