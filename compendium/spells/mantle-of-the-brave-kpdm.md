# Mantle of the Brave
*2nd-level, Abjuration*  

- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 hour

You touch up to four individuals, bolstering their courage. The next time a creature affected by this spell must make a saving throw against a spell or effect that would cause the [frightened](/compendium/rules/conditions.md#Frightened) condition, it has advantage on the roll. Once a creature has received this benefit, the spell ends for that creature.

**Classes**: [Cleric](/compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-cleric.md); [Paladin](/compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-paladin.md); [Bard](/compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md)
