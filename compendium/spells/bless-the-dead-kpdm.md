# Bless the Dead
*cantrip, Abjuration*  

- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You grant a blessing to one deceased creature, enabling it to cross over to the realm of the dead in peace. A creature that benefits from this spell can't become undead. The spell has no effect on living creatures or the undead.

**Classes**: [Druid](/compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-druid.md); [Cleric](/compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-cleric.md); [Warlock](/compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-warlock.md); [Bard](/compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md)
