# Greyfen Hollow — Map Brief

Temporary working note for drawing the player-facing map of [[Greyfen Hollow]]. Dimensions and orientation are new (not canon); everything else is from the location note.

## Mapping conventions
- **Scale:** 5-ft grid. **North = up**; the complex goes deeper as you move south. The deep level curls west so D3 can sit behind the Reservoir's north wall without hitting the Foundry.
- **Two maps:** surface (A1–A2) and caves (B1–D3). The Gullet is the only link between them.
- **Keep off the player version** until discovered: the B2 crack into the Sunken Passage, and the D3 slab and Cold Room.
- **Rename D2 on the player map.** "Izek's Watch" gives the twist away; use "The Overlook" or leave it unlabeled.

---

## Surface map

### A1 — The Drowned Threshold (outdoor)
- **Size:** about 200 × 200 ft of black water with dead grey trunks every 5–10 ft. Every tile is difficult terrain.
- **The route in:** one poleable channel, about 10 ft wide, winds in from the north edge. Draw it clearer and slightly lighter than the surrounding water.
- **The rise:** a dry oval of stone and hard earth in the center, about 60 × 45 ft, 3–4 ft above the water.
- **Sinkhole silt:** a 10-ft band all around the rise except where the channel lands. Draw it as flat, smooth mud that looks safe.
- **The Warden:** stands on the rise between the landing and the door.

### A2 — The Cap
- **Building:** squat stone house on the rise, 30 × 25 ft outside, 5-ft walls — a single room about 20 × 15 ft inside.
- **Door:** one doorway facing north toward the landing.
- **East wall:** long workbench with tools and alchemical apparatus.
- **West wall:** rack of inert amber cores.
- **The chair:** an oversized chair at the bench. Draw it large; leave the claw gouges out.
- **Hatch:** 5-ft hatch at the south end under an iron grate. Mark it clearly — it's the hook.

## Cave map

### B1 — The Gullet
- **Shape:** round shaft about 15 ft across and 40 ft deep. On a floor plan, a circle with switchback stair treads around the edge, labeled "↓ 40 ft."
- **Optional side view:** an inset shows the depth better.
- **Exit:** bottom landing opens south into B2.

### B2 — The Weeping Gallery (hub)
- **Shape:** irregular cave about 60 × 50 ft, 25–30 ft ceiling.
- **Flowstone curtains:** 4–5 hanging to within a few feet of the floor, drawn as wavy lines. They block line of sight but not movement.
- **Floor:** puddles and drip pools everywhere.
- **Exits:**
	- north: the Gullet
	- southwest: 10-ft tool-cut passage with lantern hooks, to B3
	- southeast, behind a thick flowstone column: 2–3 ft wet crack to B4 (**DM map only**)

### B3 — The Cistern
- **Size:** about 70 × 60 ft.
- **The pool:** round, dark, about 35 ft across and deep, with a 10–15 ft stone ledge all the way around.
- **Living quarters on the ledge:**
	- west: 6–8 bunks
	- north: cook fire with a smoke stain on the ceiling
	- laundry lines strung across the northeast
	- card table with stools
- **Guard post (south mouth):** crate barricade on each side of a 10-ft passage south to C1; alarm bell on a post.
- **Lighting:** lanterns every 15 ft or so. The whole ledge is lit — that open lit floor is what makes crossing it hard.

### B4 — The Sunken Passage
- **Shape:** winding crawl about 120 ft long, 3–5 ft wide, low ceiling.
- **Flooded section:** the middle 60 ft is underwater to the ceiling. Shade it blue.
- **Squeeze point:** near the far end, about 18 inches wide.
- **Exit:** comes out behind the crate stacks on C1's east wall.

### C1 — The Sorting Floor
- **Shape:** squared-off, partly worked cave about 50 × 40 ft, leveled floor.
- **Entrances:** from B3 at the north end of the west side; B4 comes in behind the crates on the east wall.
- **Crate stacks:** 5-ft stacks piled 2–3 high along the east wall, forming two or three aisles.
- **Clerk's desk:** tall desk near the center facing the entrance, with a chained ledger.
- **South wall:** racks of items waiting to be processed.
- **Exit:** wide 15-ft opening south into C2.

### C2 — The Distillery
- **Size:** about 80 × 50 ft, 35–40 ft ceiling.
- **Extraction cradles:** three rows running east–west, five per row, each about 5 × 5 ft, 10-ft aisles between rows.
- **Pipework:** copper pipes from every cradle into a main line along the south wall, heading down toward D1.
- **Catwalks:** two levels, at 15 ft and 25 ft, running the length of the room with ladders at each end. Draw as dashed or hatched overlays. Both levels continue into C3.
- **Exits:**
	- north: opening to C1
	- south: floor-level archway into C3, plus the catwalk crossing over it

### C3 — The Foundry
- **Size:** about 70 × 60 ft, 40-ft ceiling.
- **Six Warden cradles:** iron frames about 10 × 10 ft each, drawn as heavy cover.
	- three nearly empty (just legs and a spine)
	- two almost complete
	- the finished Warden's cradle against the south wall
- **Feed lines:** three glowing lines across the floor from a channel in the southwest corner to the finished Warden's cradle. Color them amber — they're what the fight is about.
- **Conduit-setting bench:** along the east wall.
- **Catwalks:** both levels wrap three sides of the room; 15-ft drop from the lower level.
- **Exit:** passage in the southwest corner drops about 20 ft over 50 ft, curving west into D1. The feed lines run along it.

### D1 — The Reservoir
- **Shape:** round, domed cave about 60 ft across, 40-ft dome. Enter from the east.
- **Basin:** shallow, about 30 ft across, filled with glowing amber liquid.
- **Glyph-stones:** six standing stones about 6 ft tall, evenly spaced in a hexagon about 5 ft outside the basin's edge.
- **Cask pallets:** stacked along the west wall.
- **South wall:** rock-cut stair climbing to an opening 20 ft up (D2).
- **North wall:** plain rock on the player map. DM map shows a 5-ft counterweighted slab and a dry patch of wall.

### D2 — Izek's Watch (player label: "The Overlook")
- **Shape:** rock ledge-room about 15 × 20 ft, 20 ft above D1. The side facing D1 is open, no railing, looking down over the basin.
- **Contents:** cot, whetstone, bottle, battleaxe leaning within reach of the cot.
- **Shelf:** on the back wall. Whether the carvings are there is still the DM's call (see the warning in [[Greyfen Hollow]]).

### D3 — The Cold Room (DM map only)
- **Location:** behind the slab in D1's north wall.
- **Shape:** about 20 × 15 ft, low 8-ft ceiling.
- **Contents:** straw-lined racks floor to ceiling on three walls holding the finished conduits; a few casks with the courier's sigil by the slab.
